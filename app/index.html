FIRST: WHAT COUNTS AS ATTENTION?

Stars rate the restaurant.
These buttons react to the review.

Useful

The reader marks the review as helpful information.

Funny

The reader marks the review as amusing.

Cool

A broad positive reaction to the review. Not a rating of the restaurant.

A bad experience can make a useful review. Useful does not mean “good restaurant”; Funny does not mean “happy customer”.

How we count these votes

Illustration only: 3 Useful + 1 Funny + 0 Cool = 4 votes on one review. It counts as one review with a reaction. Four votes do not necessarily mean four different people.

The method, limits & AI record

Definitions. Negative = 1–2 stars; positive = 4–5 stars; 3 stars remain visible as a middle category. All votes = Useful + Funny + Cool, a sum of votes, not unique people. Reach = reviews with ≥1 vote / reviews. Mean = total votes / reviews; median is calculated from the full vote-count distribution.

Data quality. Join reviews to businesses on business_id. Exact Restaurants category token; BC only; open and closed businesses included. Numeric vote strings are validated as nonnegative integers. Zero is observed no votes; null/invalid values are excluded, never converted to zero. Review IDs are checked for duplicates; review_full is checked for coverage and matching analytical fields, never stacked onto reviews.

Exposure & selection. Votes are cumulative snapshot counts; vote timestamps and exposure are absent. Older reviews had longer to accumulate votes. The year filter is a cohort check, not an age-adjusted causal model. Reviewer activity, restaurant mix, ranking, text length and other confounders remain. This historical, city-label subset is not a representative sample of every restaurant or customer in Metro Vancouver.

Reproducibility. This app contains aggregate counts only, loads without internet, and recomputes every chart and narrative from the same filtered aggregates. Audit & source hashes · Default rating summary · AI-use disclosure.
